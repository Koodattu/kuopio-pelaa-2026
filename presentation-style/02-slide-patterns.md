# 02 · Slide patterns

## Anatomy of a content slide

Almost every content slide follows the same four parts:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ TITLE: a claim or a question            (Arial Black 32) │
│ Subtitle: sets up or twists the title        (grey 18)   │
│                                                          │
│                 EVIDENCE                                 │
│   chart · quote card · screenshot · comparison · number  │
│                                                          │
│ Punchline sentence, or a question to the room   (bold)   │
│ Lähde: Source A · Source B · Source C           (small)  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Examples from the original deck:

| Title | Subtitle | Bottom line |
|---|---|---|
| Tekoälyllä tuotettu materiaali poistettiin | …mutta vaikuttiko se? Poistuiko epäluulo? | Jos AI-sisältö poistetaan, muuttaako se sinun suhtautumistasi? |
| Tekoälyn pelisääntöjä etsitään yhä | Valven linja Steamissa on vaihtunut… | Kehitys ei odottanut, että säännöt valmistuvat. |
| Pelejä tulee olemaan paljon enemmän | Tuleeko "AI-Slop" pelit täyttämään joka paikan? | Tunnit pelaajien päivissä eivät lisäänny. |

**Rule:** if you cannot write the bottom line, the slide does not yet know
what it is for.

## Catalogue of slide types

### Frame slides

**1. Collage title.** Ransom-note title words, speaker cut-out, mascot,
corkboard. No other text.

**2. Agenda corkboard.** The title shrinks to the top, and the questions the
talk will answer appear as pinned sticky notes. The central question gets the
salmon note. A follow-up build can regroup the notes into the two halves of
the event (e.g. "Hei peliala, missä mennään? (kiinnostavaa kuultavaa)" /
"Protoillaan omia pelejä (hauskaa tekemistä)").
*Reuse tip:* bring this board back at each section break with the current
note highlighted.

**3. Bio on cardboard.** Taped paper labels stacked as a career ladder (role,
organisation in small type), a photo stack on the right, and one self-ironic
last line ("Moon härmästä").

**4. Permission slide.** Mascot or character + "Saa kysyä! Saa keskeyttää!" in
the playful display font.

**5. Takeaways corkboard.** 3–6 sticky notes, one takeaway each.

**6. Closing corkboard.** Speaker cut-out, the final 3 takeaways as pinned
notes, contact cards (LinkedIn, social handle, website, email), mascot.

### Audience prompts

**7. Word association.** "Mitä teille tulee mieleen sanasta **X**?" with a big
question mark. Collect 2–4 shouts.

**8. Poll / ask-the-room.** Playful display font ("kysytään yleisöltä") with an
icon; the actual poll runs in a separate tool.

**9. "Would you?" grid.** A grid of 4–6 thumbnails + "Pelaisitko sinä näitä?"
followed by individual full-bleed videos/screens.

### Evidence slides

**10. Progressive chart.** The same chart on consecutive slides: empty axes →
first data → full data → regrouped by colour. Ask the room to guess at the
empty stage. Keep the title, subtitle and source identical across the steps so
only the data changes.

**11. Big number.** One or two huge blue figures with short labels
("47 % käyttää tekoälyä koodaamiseen"). Bottom line interprets them.

**12. Stat bar.** A single 100 % stacked bar with the headline percentage above
it ("85 % suhtautui kielteisesti") and a three-group legend below.

**13. Quote card grid.** 2–4 light cards: product/game name small on top, the
quote in quotation marks, person · organisation underneath. Highlight the key
phrase in blue.

**14. Headline screenshot.** Real news headline or social post as the
evidence, cropped tight, sometimes 2–3 side by side.

**15. Contradiction pair.** Two statements from the same organisation side by
side (the product-level promise vs. the company-level practice), with a
deadpan line underneath ("Tietenkin… ovat eri asioita, mutta…").

### Explanation slides

**16. Two-column definition.** Two concepts side by side, each with a name, a
quoted user intent ("Mitä tässä on?" / "Tee minulle tällainen."), a one-line
explanation and a tiny input→output example image.

**17. Three-box framework.** Three coloured boxes in a triangle or row, with
1–3 questions under each (e.g. Omaperäisyys · Laatu · Tekotapa). Optionally a
centre element that a later build replaces (Tekoäly → ChatGPT logo).

**18. Three-point timeline.** `2023 → 2024 → Nyt` (or similar), a dot per
point, a bold one-line state, and one or two lines of detail. Punchline at the
bottom.

**19. Comparison table.** Rows = dimensions, columns = old thing vs. new thing
(asset flip vs. AI slop). Shows the new problem is an old pattern.

**20. Domain card.** For a sub-area of the topic: title = the sub-area,
subtitle = what it covers, left = "what it can already do" (demo, example),
right/bottom = "where it falls short", punchline at the bottom.

**21. Loop diagram.** A small cycle (Idea → Prototyyppi → Pelitestaus → "Ei
ollut hauskaa" → Toista) with the takeaway beside it.

### Story slides

**22. Case opener.** Full-bleed key art, credit only.

**23. Case beat.** Faded key art behind, title = the beat ("Tätä peliä
odotettiin", "Sitten puhe kääntyi tekoälyyn"), two quote cards or a short
timeline, question at the bottom. A case is 2–4 beats.

**24. Historical reframe.** "Ollaanko tässä oltu ennenkin?" + a then/now
pair of images with years ("1835: Kangas syntyy koneella." / "2026: Peli syntyy
pyynnöstä.").

### Punctuation

**25. Full-slide meme.** No title. One per joke; use them between sections, not
inside an argument.

**26. Full-bleed video.** A demo or clip, no title, plays on click.

### Workshop slides

**27. Workshop intro.** Why the hands-on part exists ("Minusta tekoälyn paras
käyttötapa on…") with a visual of iterations (Kokeilu 1 … Kokeilu 5 · Tämä!).

**28. Objection pre-empt.** Answer the obvious objection before it is asked
(energy use, cost, privacy) with a small bar chart and explicit assumptions.

**29. Workshop instructions.** A numbered loop in coloured boxes (0. Aloita →
1. Pelaa ja testaa → 2. Tee pieni muutos → 3. Uusi idea), plus a QR code, URL
and access code.
