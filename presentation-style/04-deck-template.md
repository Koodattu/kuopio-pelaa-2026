# 04 · Deck template

A topic-agnostic arc for a **60-minute talk + break + hands-on workshop**.
Replace `X` with the topic and `<field>` with the audience's world (games,
music, healthcare, schools…).

## Slide budget

- Aim for **one new point per 40–60 seconds**. For 60 minutes that is roughly
  **60–75 slides**, not counting progressive-reveal builds and full-slide
  memes.
- Interaction (polls, shouts, questions) costs 5–10 minutes. Budget it.
- If a section runs long, cut a case study before cutting interaction.

## The arc

| # | Beat | Time | Slide types (see [02](02-slide-patterns.md)) | Write down |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | 1 min | Collage title → agenda corkboard | The provocative title; 5–7 questions the talk answers; the central question on the salmon note. |
| 2 | **Who I am** | 1–2 min | Bio on cardboard → permission slide | Career ladder, one photo, one self-ironic line. |
| 3 | **Warm-up** | 2 min | Word association | "Mitä teille tulee mieleen sanasta X?" |
| 4 | **Basics: what X is, why now** | 4–5 min | Two-column definition, short history (2–3 images), three-box "why now" | The one distinction people confuse; the 3 forces that made X happen now. |
| 5 | **What the professionals think** | 4 min | Poll → progressive chart, big number | One survey with a trend over time; one "how they actually use it" stat. |
| 6 | **What the public / users think** | 3 min | Stat bar, progressive chart | The headline % and the most interesting split (what they accept vs. reject). |
| 7 | **Why opinions differ** | 3 min | Country/segment chart, three-column explanation | The cultural or economic reason; tie it back to `<field>`. |
| 8 | **Controversy cases** | 8 min | Case opener → case beats (×2 cases max), quote grid, contradiction pair, then framework | Two real stories, one "said vs. did" moment, one framework slide that makes sense of them. |
| 9 | **Domain by domain** | 10 min | Section opener, domain cards (one per sub-area), loop diagram | For each sub-area: what X can already do, where it falls short. Balance time across sub-areas. |
| 10 | **What's possible now** | 5 min | "Would you?" grid → 3 videos → reflection slide | Three strongest demos; the "first impression vs. reason to return" point. |
| 11 | **"Been here before"** | 4 min | Historical reframe, comparison table, examples built with older tools | The pre-X version of today's worry; examples of great work made with "shortcut" tools. |
| 12 | **Opportunity turn** | 2 min | "Nyt on paras aika…" | The invitation; the lowered barrier. |
| 13 | **Answer the title** | 2 min | Historical reframe / emotional pair | Who loses, who gains, what changes for the next generation. |
| 14 | **Personal proof** | 5 min | 3–4 slides: what it is & why, **what X did vs. what I decided**, best visual, QR | The speaker's own project as the worked example. |
| 15 | **Takeaways** | 1 min | Takeaways corkboard (3 notes) | The three sentences you want quoted afterwards. |
| — | **Break** | 10–15 min | Workshop slide with QR left on screen | |
| 16 | **Workshop** | 40–45 min | Workshop intro → objection pre-empt → instructions | The loop the participants follow; URL, QR, access code. |
| 17 | **Close** | 5 min | Feedback prompt → closing corkboard | Optional: repeat the opening poll. Contacts. |

## Section returns

With 60+ slides, re-show the agenda corkboard at the start of beats 4, 8, 9,
11 and 14 with the current question highlighted. It costs five slides and
saves the audience from getting lost.

## Variations

- **30 minutes, no workshop:** beats 1–3 (compressed), 4, 5+6 merged, 8 (one
  case), 9 (three sub-areas), 11, 13, 15, close. ~35 slides.
- **90 minutes talk only:** add a third case to beat 8, a live demo to beat 10
  and a Q&A block before beat 15.
- **Workshop-first:** beats 1–3, 16, then a short version of 9–13 interpreting
  what the participants just experienced.
